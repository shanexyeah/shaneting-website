About Shane

Built to challenge the room.

Shane Ting is a professional public speaker, strategist, and thought leader who has spent over a decade helping organizations unlock their potential through the power of clear communication, authentic leadership, and courageous culture.

From boardrooms to arena stages, Shane brings a rare combination of research-backed insights and raw, human storytelling. Audiences don't just hear Shane — they feel moved to act differently.

Based in Sydney, Australia, Shane has spoken in over 40 countries for clients including Google, TEDx, Microsoft, Atlassian, and the United Nations.
