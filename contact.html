Common questions

How far in advance should I book?

Ideally 3–6 months ahead for large events. For smaller events, 4–8 weeks may be possible — reach out and I'll let you know my availability.

Do you travel internationally?

Yes — I speak globally. Travel costs are typically covered by the client. Virtual options are also available worldwide.

Can talks be customised?

Every engagement is tailored. I research your audience, industry, and goals ahead of time to ensure the content lands perfectly.

What formats are available?

Keynotes (30–90 min), half-day and full-day workshops, panel moderation, fireside chats, and multi-session leadership programs.
